Joey Budi

I'm Joshua (Joey) Budi, an agronomy data analyst with about 10 years of experience bringing data science and engineering to agriculture. My work has spanned commercial ag business, product insights, sustainability, and large- and small-scale agronomy research in the US Midwest and in Indonesia. I live in Tangerang, Indonesia.

Projects

Notebooks from my work, rebuilt on simulated data so the methods can be shared without the data.

  • Predicting next season's summer crop XGBoost on one-year-lagged field records, benchmarked against a crop rotation rule.
  • Matching trial-site growers to customer records Fuzzy name and address scores plus distance to link records with no shared ID, with a map of the results.
  • Retail footprint and disease-weather overlay Retailer and county rollups, a county choropleth, and a spatial join to a weather grid for fungicide demand.
  • Private label vs. flagship brand share Pairing equivalent products by naming rules to map each retailer's private-label share.
  • Renewable fuel plants: from web maps to GeoDataFrame Scraping embedded map JSON, mapping ethanol, biodiesel and biomass plants, and county access to ethanol demand.
  • Forecasting planting dates from field history XGBoost on relative-lag field history, soils and spring weather, validated forward in time against simple baselines.
  • Matching product names to an item master: four methods compared Rules, n-grams, learned code cores and a LightGBM classifier, measured against known truth. Built with substantial AI assistance.
  • Herbicide-trait shifts in seed sales Mapping seed traits to herbicide systems and tracking share changes by state, with choropleths and trend lines.

I analyze nationwide agronomy research plots, retail sales, carbon-market data, and public datasets for commercial, product, and sustainability teams. My toolkit includes regression, clustering, XGBoost, and time-series models, along with geospatial work joining soil, weather, and watershed data (Python, R, SQL, Sedona, ArcGIS). I also build ETL pipelines in Databricks and share results through Power BI dashboards.

Before that I worked in precision agriculture at Premier Crop Systems on variable-rate seeding, fertilizer prescriptions, and ROI analyses, and at SoilView doing GPS-guided grid soil sampling in central Iowa.

Since 2026 I've been a farm information systems consultant for PT Salam Growing Centers, an organic vegetable farm in Tasikmalaya, West Java, where I advise on data practices for decision-making.

Research

For my M.S. in Sustainable Agriculture at Iowa State University (2020, minors in Statistics and Plant Pathology), I studied how diversified cropping systems, cover crops, and seed treatments affect sudden death syndrome of soybean, caused by Fusarium virguliforme. I worked with Dr. Leonor Leandro and Dr. Matt Liebman, ran 5+ field experiments, and led the economic analysis of fungicidal seed treatments for USDA-NIFA funded research. Earlier I coordinated multi-state crop phenotyping for root microbiome and photosynthesis research with the University of Texas at Austin. I hold a B.S. in Biology from Calvin University.
